# Support

Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Mason-Ye-Project/metropulse-pipeline-incident-lab/issues)
for reproducible code defects, fixture mismatches, and book-to-lab errata.

Include:

- the incident id;
- the exact command;
- `python -m metropulse_lab doctor` output;
- the release tag or commit;
- the smallest relevant evidence excerpt.

Do not post credentials, personal data, employer logs, or proprietary datasets.
The project does not provide production incident response or individualized
operational support.
